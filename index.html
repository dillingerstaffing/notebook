Design language · research edition · 16 IX 2026

The notebook is a method, not a texture.

A digital page feels like a brilliant maker’s private notebook when it preserves how thought moves: underdraw, compare, commit, revise. Paper grain and coffee stains imitate the evidence without doing the work.

The standard. Every mark must document, compare, explain, quantify, orient, or invite useful exploration. If it only says “old notebook,” erase it.

01 · What the codices actually do

A working surface for thought.

Leonardo’s pages do not feel unified because they share a visual theme. They feel unified because drawing, annotation, analogy, and revision occupy one continuous field.

A

Medium records commitment.

Iron-gall ink, made from oak gall, gum arabic, and iron sulphate, supports both fine delineation and loose exploration. Red and black chalk provide a softer working line, while black chalk can establish bold structure beneath ink. Metalpoint can sit beneath both as underdrawing. The hierarchy is functional, not decorative. [02] [03]

B

Text and diagram meet at the point of need.

The V&A describes single sheets where ideas and inventions form an unpredictable pattern, with sketches and diagrams annotated directly. Anatomical diagrams move into the margin when the analogy belongs there, as in the adjacent vertebrae and boat-mast comparison. [01] [10]

C

One subject earns one folio.

Leonardo often used a folio as a small cohesive treatise on one subject. Loose sheets were later folded, bound, cut, and rearranged. His own note calls the Arundel material a “collection without order,” copied first and arranged by subject later. [06] [07] [08]

D

Revision remains visible.

Partly erased black-chalk alternatives were reworked in ink. Scientific imaging of The Virgin of the Rocks likewise exposes changed poses, re-angled heads, and removed curls. A former answer can remain legible as evidence of changing judgment. [12] [13]

E

Comparison happens within eye-span.

A shoulder folio repeats the same anatomy from several angles and progressive dissection states, including lifted muscles, a cord-like reduction, and rotated bone. The page makes depth comparative rather than sequential. [09] [10]

F

Margins carry argument.

Writing can extend beyond what later became an inner margin, suggesting the sheet was written before folding. The boundary of the thought, not a prebuilt card grid, decided the composition. Labels key directly to nearby diagram parts. [01] [11]

G

The private register serves the hand.

Mirror writing is documented as habitual, but its explanation remains open: left-handed practicality and privacy are the principal theories. Leonardo’s letterforms were otherwise ordinary. A 2019 study reported mirror script with the left hand on one side of a sheet and ordinary left-to-right writing with the right on the other. [01] [04] [05]

H

Apparent scraps may contain the result.

Red-chalk notes once dismissed as irrelevant were later identified as an early written demonstration of laws of friction. The lesson is not to romanticize every scribble, but to preserve working evidence until its status is understood. [14]

Mirror writing is not a visual effect. The evidence supports a working habit, not a decorative device. A backwards interface would perform mystery while obstructing use.
Leader-line confidence: moderate. Direct keying is visible at folio level, but this research did not verify one primary quotation naming the convention. Do not make a historical claim stronger than the evidence.
02 · Material grammar

Three layers. Three states of certainty.

A useful notebook language does not assign colors to topics. It assigns graphic weight to the status of a thought.

Verdict retained

Use color as grammar. One dark ink for conclusions, one restrained accent for active annotation, and one graphite register for structure. More colors demand more meanings.
Interaction rule. Focus may change emphasis, but it should not erase the document. The diagram above remains informative before and after a tap.
03 · Composition grammar

Put the evidence where the eye needs it.

A working notebook is not a pile of cards. It is a field in which placement carries meaning: margin beside claim, label beside part, alternatives within eye-span.

04 · Modern precedents

Simple design. Intense content.

The strongest digital precedents preserve the working method without pretending the screen is old paper.

Tufte

Spend ink on evidence.

His analytical-design principles call for documentary, comparative, causal, explanatory, quantified, multivariate, exploratory, and skeptical displays. He argues for the complete integration of word, number, and image, with display debris removed. His concise standard is “Simple design, intense content.” [15] [16]

Distill

Evidence breaks the measure.

Prose stays readable while figures widen only when the evidence needs room. Footnotes remain attached to claims, figures can mount only when needed, and desktop sidenotes collapse inline on small screens. The journal describes its distinguishing trait as outstanding communication and dedication to human understanding. [22] [23] [25]

Matuschak

Working state stays public.

Notes are written primarily for the maker, densely linked, publicly shared as an experiment, and stamped with their last update. The notebook feeling comes from visible working state rather than a notebook costume. [27]

Red Blob Games

Useful before touched.

Amit Patel treats explainers as documents with interactive elements and avoids interactions outside the reader’s control. His stated requirement is that diagrams remain useful before interaction. [28] [29]

Research debt is design debt. Chris Olah frames better explanatory media as a way to reduce the burden created when ideas remain hard to understand. [24]
Caveat. Distill is on indefinite hiatus. Its archived conventions remain useful as a design precedent, not as evidence of an active publication.
05 · The complete design language

Surface, type, ink, and time.

The language is restrained because the content is dense. It should feel authored by the same mind at every scale, from a citation mark to a full-page diagram.

Ground

One flat sheet.

Use a warm neutral field with no paper grain, gradients, shadows, torn edges, or staged aging. The page edge is the primary frame. Hairlines may divide registers when they also clarify structure.

paper / field
ink / conclusion
sanguine / work
graphite / underdraw
Typography

Reading voice plus instrument voice.

Use a high-legibility serif for explanation and a compact monospaced face for folio numbers, measurements, dates, controls, and labels. Never use a handwriting font to manufacture authenticity.

Hierarchy

Scale follows certainty.

The current conclusion receives the largest reading type. Evidence is smaller and nearby. Provenance and caveats use the margin register. Uppercase belongs to brief technical labels, not long prose.

Line

Every stroke has a verb.

A line connects, measures, divides, revises, or directs attention. A border that merely decorates a container is suspect. Leader lines end at visible targets and are recalculated with the rendered layout.

Spacing

Whitespace groups, it does not pad.

Keep claim and evidence close. Give topic changes a full folio break. Let figures widen only when comparison or legibility requires it. Avoid repeated card gutters that fragment one thought into unrelated tiles.

Motion

State change, not theater.

Motion may preserve object constancy, confirm an input, orient the eye, or reveal a mechanism. It must be interruptible, brief, and absent under reduced-motion preferences. If the static frame is meaningless, animation does not rescue it.

Interaction

Manipulate premises, not chrome.

Controls should change a meaningful relation, comparison, or level of evidence. The answer remains visible before interaction. Repeated input records a revision rather than faking persistence or success.

Responsive

Recompose, never miniaturize.

At phone width, margins move inline, columns stack, and labels remain attached to their subjects. No horizontal scroll. Hover can enrich, but never carry meaning unavailable to touch or keyboard users.

Accessibility

Meaning survives color and motion.

Use text labels, weight, position, and line form alongside color. Provide visible focus, native controls, descriptive alternative text, and sufficient contrast in both system color schemes.

Data-ink test. Ask what understanding disappears if a mark is erased. If the answer is “none,” erase it.
Do not literalize the medium. The aim is to reproduce working behavior, not parchment. Digital affordances remain honest: selectable text, responsive layout, direct manipulation, and links.
06 · Reusable patterns

Nine devices that earn their place.

These patterns form a system. Use only the ones the content can justify.

01

Folio-treatise

Give one subject one continuous page. The reader can later order or link folios, but each one should stand as a complete act of thought.

02

Commitment layers

Separate underdrawing, working marks, and retained conclusions through weight and role. Do not use accent colors as decoration.

03

Evidence margin

Place sources, caveats, and analogous diagrams beside the claim they qualify. Collapse them inline on narrow screens.

04

Visible revision

Retain superseded readings as dated, struck notes when the change itself is informative. Preserve uncertainty instead of manufacturing omniscience.

05

Direct keying

Put labels on the figure or connect them to exact parts with short, stable lines. Avoid distant legends that create eye travel.

06

Small multiples

Repeat the same format and scale while varying one meaningful state. Comparison then happens in space, not from memory.

07

Answer-first disclosure

Show the conclusion and its essential basis immediately. Interaction may reveal more evidence, never gate the answer.

08

Word-sized evidence

Use tiny inline measures, bars, or status marks when they clarify a claim without creating a separate chart zone. A sparkline is useful because it fits the sentence it informs.

09

Skeptical controls

Let readers test premises, compare alternatives, or inspect how a verdict changes. A control that only triggers prose or decoration does not qualify.

08 · The quality gate

Keep the work. Cut the costume.

Skeuomorphism earns its place only when the interface behaves meaningfully like the object it evokes. Otherwise, the notebook becomes set dressing. [18] [19]

Keep

  • One subject per folio
  • Margins that carry evidence
  • Direct labels and finite leader lines
  • Visible revision and uncertainty
  • Small multiples for comparison
  • Interaction that reveals mechanism
  • Flat responsive composition
  • Conclusions useful before touch

Cut

  • Coffee stains and torn edges
  • Washi tape and faux aging
  • Handwriting fonts for interface copy
  • Ruled-paper backgrounds
  • Page curls and reveal theater
  • Icons without an educational job
  • Controls that only print commentary
  • Animation outside reader control
The hard test. If removing a mark loses no evidence, comparison, orientation, control, or legibility, remove it.
The counterpoint. A physical metaphor can carry feeling when its behavior is useful. Paper by FiftyThree was praised for page-turning that made interaction feel warm and human. The value was the interaction, not a stain. [20]
Responsive honesty. Decorative physical metaphors often fail when reduced to a small screen. A digital page should use the screen’s real strengths rather than inherit paper’s constraints. [19]
09 · Build audit

Nine proofs before release.

I
Static truthThe conclusion and every diagram’s baseline meaning are visible before interaction.
II
AdjacencyNo legend, source, or control asks the eye to travel farther than its subject.
III
Ink economyEach color, line, enclosure, and icon has one defined informational role.
IV
ComparisonAlternatives share a format and remain visible together when comparison matters.
V
Visible correctionMeaningful re-analysis records the former judgment instead of erasing the trail.
VI
Interaction valueEvery control changes a relation, reveals evidence, improves orientation, or disappears.
VII
Motion restraintMotion is reader-controlled, survives reduced-motion settings, and adds information.
VIII
Phone integrityMargins become inline notes, comparisons stack, and nothing scrolls sideways.

ProvenanceClaims distinguish live-verified sources, indexed text, secondary summaries, and open questions.
